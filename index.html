Where prices come from

ComicScan checks these shops each time you look a comic up and lists what they're selling it for right now, cheapest first:

  • Midtown Comics: new and back issues, slabs
  • Comic Source (formerly Newkadia): back issues by grade
  • Infinity Flux: new releases and variants
  • InStockTrades: trade paperbacks and hardcovers

MyComicShop (which doesn't allow automatic checks), Amazon, eBay sold listings, PriceCharting and GoCollect are one tap away on each comic's page. ComicScan is free, with no account needed.

Privacy

The camera picture is read on your phone and never saved or uploaded. Only the barcode number or the title you search for is sent, to look the comic up.
